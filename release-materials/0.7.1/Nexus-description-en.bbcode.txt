[center][size=6][b]FACE LIGHTING[/b][/size]
[color=#C9AA72]Player & dialogue NPC lighting.[/color]

Adjustable face lighting for you and the people you talk to.[/center]

Add a gentle fill light to the player's face or your current dialogue partner. Tune each light separately, preview changes in-game, and keep the look that suits your setup.

[size=4][color=#C9AA72][b]Two lights. Your settings.[/b][/color][/size]
[list]
[*][b]Player face light[/b] — Toggle with [b]L[/b]. Off by default, with an adjustable shortcut. Visible in third person.
[*][b]Dialogue face light[/b] — Automatically lights the current dialogue partner in first or third person. Optional fade-in and fade-out, with adjustable duration.
[*][b]Independent controls[/b] — Adjust intensity, position, and color temperature from [b]2000–10000 K[/b]. Set the radius in regular mode, or use CS inverse-square falloff with a range derived from intensity.
[*][b]Live configuration[/b] — Three pages: General, Player face light, and NPC face light. English and Simplified Chinese, with automatic Windows language detection.
[/list]

[size=4][color=#C9AA72][b]Get started[/b][/color][/size]
Install with your mod manager and launch through SKSE. No ESP or Papyrus scripts are included.

[b]Required[/b]
[url=https://skse.silverlock.org/]SKSE64[/url] and [url=https://www.nexusmods.com/skyrimspecialedition/mods/32444]Address Library[/url], matching your game version.

[b]For the in-game menu[/b]
[url=https://www.nexusmods.com/skyrimspecialedition/mods/120352]SKSE Menu Framework[/url]. You can also edit [b]Data/SKSE/Plugins/FaceLighting.ini[/b] and restart.

Open [b]FaceLighting[/b] in the framework. Changes preview immediately and remain while switching pages. [b]Save all settings[/b] keeps them; closing the menu discards unsaved edits. Keep your existing INI when upgrading.

[size=4][color=#C9AA72][b]Community Shaders[/b][/color][/size]
CS is optional. Recognized builds offer inverse-square falloff with automatic linear-light flags and calibrated intensity. Regular lighting remains available without CS.

[b]One setup step:[/b] in General, match [b]“My CS global Linear Lighting is enabled”[/b] to your actual CS setting. This selects the face lights' color handling; it does not turn on CS. Update it whenever you change the global CS option.

[size=4][color=#C9AA72][b]Compatibility[/b][/color][/size]
[b]Skyrim 1.5.97 has passed in-game testing.[/b] The plugin is built for SE/AE; use dependencies matching your runtime. VR is not supported.

CS testing primarily used a [b]Jiaye experimental build[/b]. The recognized protocol families are CS 1.6.0 / 1.8.4 with ISL 1-3-0 metadata; this does not mean every build has been tested. ENB is not yet validated.

Lights are shadowless and can also illuminate nearby objects. Actors need a standard head node. Dialogue detection uses Skyrim's standard dialogue menu and speaker; replacement dialogue systems may need separate support. Persistent lighting for manually selected NPCs is not included in this release.
